# Current criterion reconciliation

This maps the frozen criteria to the current implementation and tests after the
accepted change orders. The frozen plan is unchanged. C01 retains its verified
attachment disposition. C02–C50 are **evidence incomplete** against their complete
original products; none is now merely “not assessed.” The rows below state what
was actually checked and what remains, without promoting samples to exhaustive
coverage. Historical per-criterion planted-defect records remain at
[fda24282](https://github.com/ConductorOne/baton-sdk/blob/fda242827a2e177e7f459d802502fd8f4f8fbe2b/docs/verification/syncer-on-ledger/evidence.md).

- **R1 — unexecuted combinations:** mechanisms and named boundaries have tests;
  the entire operation × feature × cut × process × worker product has not run.
- **R2 — lower-level faults:** not every read, iterator-close, syscall and staging
  error has an individually injected consumer test. Engine and public fault
  tests cover complementary layers; neither is presented as the other.
- **R3 — scale/cost:** practical collection cost is accepted under CO020; the
  original full matrix remains incomplete. Count bounds are not byte bounds.
- **R4 — verification completeness:** current tests/source are mapped, but the
  full executed-cell/branch manifest and every final-revision mutant are not
  complete. The statement-range triage is a gap inventory, not a percentage target.
